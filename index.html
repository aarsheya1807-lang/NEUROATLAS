Methodology & References

This atlas is an educational reference tool, not a diagnostic device. Structural geometry is stylized/procedural, not derived from patient imaging.

Diagnostic classification American Psychiatric Association. Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders, 5th ed., Text Revision (DSM-5-TR). 2022.
World Health Organization. International Classification of Diseases, 11th Revision (ICD-11). 2019/2022. Neuroanatomy & function Kandel, E.R., et al. Principles of Neural Science, 6th ed. McGraw-Hill, 2021.
Purves, D., et al. Neuroscience, 6th ed. Oxford University Press, 2018. Culture-bound distress idioms DSM-5-TR, "Cultural Formulation" appendix (Glossary of Cultural Concepts of Distress).
Kirmayer, L.J. & Young, A. "Culture and Somatization." Psychosomatic Medicine, 1998. Known limitations Brain geometry is a stylized procedural mesh for orientation purposes, not a scanned or MRI-derived model. Functional-network and slice-plane views are schematic illustrations of established concepts, not real patient or population imaging data. Region boundaries (especially within the medial-orbital prefrontal cluster) are simplified; real cytoarchitectural boundaries are more gradual and debated in the literature.
