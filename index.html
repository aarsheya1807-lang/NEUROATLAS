Nucleus Accumbens

Healthy State

Anatomical & Neurochemical Profile

Key node of the ventral striatum. Receives dense dopaminergic projections from the Ventral Tegmental Area (VTA) via the mesolimbic pathway.

Clinical Function & Cognitive Role

Mediates reward prediction error, incentive salience ("wanting"), reinforcement learning, and goal-directed behavioral initiation.

DSM-5-TR & ICD-11 Classifications

Substance-Related and Addictive Disorders (DSM-5-TR) / Disorders due to substance use or addictive behaviours (ICD-11: 6C40-6C4E).

Case Diagnosis Challenge

Patient presents with expressive aphasia, non-fluent speech, but intact language comprehension.
