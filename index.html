Sources & educational disclaimer

Educational use only. Neuro Atlas is a learning tool. It does not diagnose, treat, or replace clinical judgment. Real lesions follow vascular supply and white-matter tracts, vary between people, and change with plasticity; the 3D structures here are schematic spheres, not anatomically accurate segmentations.

How to read the data

  • Function / lesion / vascular data reflect standard neuroanatomy teaching and are simplified (e.g., "Prefrontal cortex" merges several distinct subregions).
  • DSM-5-TR / ICD-11 links are associations, not one-to-one causal mappings. Most psychiatric diagnoses involve distributed networks, not a single structure. Verify codes against the current manuals before citing.
  • Cultural context entries are illustrative. Cultural idioms of distress are well documented; links to specific neural substrates are hypothesis-level and are labelled so.
  • Lesion simulator uses sphere-overlap geometry as a teaching estimate; it ignores fibre tracts, vascular territories, and individual variation.
  • Quiz & cases are for self-study. Generated questions are auto-built from the region database; hand-written cases carry fuller explanations.
